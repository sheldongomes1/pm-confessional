  ---
  Why deterministic refusal > Gemini-generated refusal
  
  The prompt makes the refusal a hardcoded server template, not a Gemini call. Three reasons worth remembering for interview prep:

  1. It's faster. Refusal is instant. No 1-2s model latency for a response that's the same every time.
  2. It's cheaper. No Gemini tokens spent on a no-op.
  3. It's reliable. The model can't accidentally hallucinate marathon-running advice into the refusal. The refusal is the safety mechanism.

  This is a small but interview-defendable architectural call: don't use an LLM for a deterministic decision. A senior PM hears that and immediately recognizes the discipline — using the right tool for each step
   of the pipeline, not just defaulting to "throw it at Gemini."
