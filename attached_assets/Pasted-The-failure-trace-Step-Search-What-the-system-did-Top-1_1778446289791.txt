  The coach has a thin disclaimer ("this is a personal decision") then proceeds anyway. Worse, the agent's clarifying questions ("What's driving your interest in running the marathon?") imply the archive has 
  marathon-running data. It doesn't. That's the credibility hit you noticed.

  Where it broke, structurally

  Two missing guardrails:

  1. The search-side low_confidence flag is computed but unused downstream. The UI knows to say "NO EXACT MATCH" but the Ask-the-Room flow ignores it.
  2. The coach's system prompt has no scope check. It assumes any question that arrives at it is a product/business decision worth coaching.

  The fix is server-side and cheap. Here's the prompt:

  ---

  Goal: stop the Decision Coach from improvising advice when the retrieval
  is low-confidence or the question is out-of-scope. We just hit a real
  failure: a user asked "should I run the NYC marathon" and the coach pulled
  5 unrelated confessions (burnout, imposter syndrome, stage fright) and
  proceeded with a normal PM-coaching script, even asking clarifying questions
  about marathon training. That destroys trust in the entire product.

  Step 1 — Compute a confidence signal at retrieval time, not at coach time.
  On the search response, add a server-computed field:

    retrieval_confidence: "high" | "medium" | "low"

    high   → top1_cosine >= 0.70 AND (no rerank ran OR top1_rerank_score >= 7)
    medium → 0.55 <= top1_cosine < 0.70 OR top1_rerank_score in [5, 7)
    low    → top1_cosine < 0.55 OR top1_rerank_score < 5

  The "should I run the NYC marathon" query (cosine 0.60, presumably low rerank
  scores across the board) should bucket as "low".

  Pass retrieval_confidence into the coach session payload alongside the
  5 source confessions.

  Step 2 — Gate the Ask the Room button on retrieval_confidence.
  On the search results page:

    high   → button reads "ASK THE ROOM" (current behavior)
    medium → button reads "ASK THE ROOM" with a small italic note below:
             "Match is loose — coach will flag if it can't ground advice."
    low    → button is still clickable, but reads "ASK ANYWAY" and shows
             a small italic note: "The archive doesn't have a strong match
             for this question. Coach will explain instead of advising."

  Don't disable the button entirely. Users may still want to see the coach's
  explanation of why their question isn't covered.
